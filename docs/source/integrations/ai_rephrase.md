# 1. AI Rephrase
With AI Rephrase you could select any text inside the editor and ask Ollama model to paraphrase the text. Simply hit "options" gear icon on the right side of the right mouse button and enter address and port where your AI model provider (right now it's Ollama) waiting for the message.

It can be Ollama on your local PC, or in your network. Simply enter the "ip:port" address in the settings, select the text and hit "AI Rephrase".

You can ask to re-phrase the text multiple times and select the best one or leave the original one. Here is the example:
